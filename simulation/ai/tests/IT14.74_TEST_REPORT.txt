IT14.74 strategy / food-layout pass — built directly from IT14.73

1. Four equal Expert doctrines
   - Early P1 Rush: 25%
   - Late P1 Timing Rush: 25%
   - P2 Forge-Tech Push: 25%
   - P3 Boom Max-Tech All-In: 25%

2. P2/P3 Civic Centre production invariant
   - P2 Tech Push and P3 Boom keep the Civic Centre on permanent civilians until 70 civilians actually exist.
   - Reaching Town/City early does not relax that rule: if Town is reached at 60 civilians, the next 10 CC units are still civilians.
   - Barracks remain the continuous citizen-soldier production engine.
   - CC hunting cavalry is also suppressed below 70 civilians for these two boom doctrines.

3. P3 Boom Max-Tech All-In
   - Village/early Town: prioritize economy and eco research; no normal P1/P2 primary attack.
   - City transition: begin the military upgrade package while City is queued/researching.
   - City: continue relevant military attack/resistance/health/movement research until the live relevant package is exhausted.
   - Athens prioritizes Iphicrates from the Prytaneion.
   - Near operating-pop cap, prepare two real building siege units: first ram; second prefers a non-ram building siege when the live Arsenal provides one, otherwise another ram.
   - Normal launch requires City, near-max operating population, >=90 assigned army, Iphicrates for Athens, two siege, and no remaining relevant military tech/research.
   - A 20:00 anti-stall deadline may waive only the final tech-completion condition; it still requires near-max population, army, Iphicrates and siege.

4. Natural-food / permanent-farm contract
   - Opening berry Farmstead remains.
   - No new permanent Field above 40% combined usable in-territory natural food.
   - Completed + foundation + queued Fields count as existing/pending capacity.
   - Zero open field slots alone no longer authorizes another Farmstead while natural food is above 40% or a Field is pending.
   - Permanent Farmsteads target 4 nearby Fields and never degrade below 3-field emergency geometry.
   - Any non-opening natural-food Farmstead must also preserve at least 3 legal future Field slots; the opening berry dropsite remains the only geometry exception.
   - Farmstead count is capped at 3 for this pass; expected mature layout is roughly 2-3 Farmsteads / 8-12 Fields.
   - Existing-hub field placement searches out to roughly 2m border gap before another hub is considered.
   - Healthy >40% natural food satisfies the old six-Field Barracks/Town insurance checks without forcing premature Fields.

5. Validation performed before packaging
   - All JavaScript files pass node --check.
   - Doctrine selection boundary test confirms four 25% ranges and total weight 1.00.
   - fieldDemand() threshold checks: 50% -> no new Fields; 41% with one pending -> no extra Field; 40% -> transition opens.
   - Farmstead planning check confirms no fourth Farmstead is requested once three are in the pipeline.
   - Actual 0 A.D. replay validation is still required; this is the IT14.74 test package.
