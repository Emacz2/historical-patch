MAJOR TRENDS
- Strong opening/army production can still create dominant positions, but the floor is inconsistent: bad placement or logistics can erase the advantage.
- Farmstead geometry remains the most visible settlement-planning weakness; first hub must support 3-4 Fields before another hub is acceptable.
- Idle/unproductive workers are now a late-game strategic failure, not just an assignment bug; failed orders need escalation and expansion.
- Finishing logic is too cautious: a broken opponent with siege must be killed, not repeatedly reboomed.
- Resource imbalance needs a global response: barter surplus into the limiting resource and expand when local resources are exhausted.
